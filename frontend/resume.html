Resume Text Extraction

ATS Score Algorithm

Score is calculated as (Matched Skills / Total Job Skills) × 100 with bonus points for extra tech breadth.

Compensation ranges are derived from industry benchmarks for your matched role, scaled by your ATS score multiplier.
